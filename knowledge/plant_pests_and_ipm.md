# Plant Pests, Disease, and Integrated Pest Management (IPM) in Aquaponics

Growing plants attract pests — but in aquaponics you **cannot spray your way out** of them. The single most
important rule: **nothing that can reach the water is safe to assume fish-safe.**

## The constraint (why you can't use normal pesticides)
- Most synthetic insecticides are **acutely lethal to fish** at tiny concentrations.
- Even "natural" botanicals are dangerous: **pyrethrin/pyrethrum is highly toxic to fish**; **neem (azadirachtin)**
  has some aquatic toxicity — if used at all, apply as a light **foliar** spray, shielded from the water, never poured in.
- **Insecticidal soap** can be used on leaves but must be kept out of the water and rinsed carefully.
Default to **physical and biological** control; treat any chemical as a last resort applied away from the system.

## The IPM ladder (work top-down)
1. **Cultural / preventive** — good airflow, adequate plant spacing, remove dead leaves, keep the grow area clean,
   inspect new seedlings before adding them.
2. **Physical** — hand-pick pests, blast aphids off with a spray of water, yellow **sticky traps** for whiteflies and
   fungus gnats, netting/screens on greenhouse vents.
3. **Biological** — the aquaponics-friendly workhorse:
   - **Ladybugs / lacewings** for aphids.
   - ***Bacillus thuringiensis israelensis* (Bti)** for fungus-gnat larvae; **Bt kurstaki** for caterpillars — both are
     bacterial and considered safe around fish when used as directed.
   - **Predatory mites** for spider mites.
4. **Chemical (last resort)** — only fish-safe options, applied foliar and shielded from the water.

## Common pests and the tell
- **Aphids** — clusters on new growth and leaf undersides; sticky honeydew. Most common.
- **Whiteflies** — tiny white flies that puff up when the plant is disturbed.
- **Spider mites** — fine webbing, stippled/speckled leaves; thrive in hot, dry air.
- **Fungus gnats** — small black flies around the media; larvae damage roots. Sticky traps + Bti.

## Common plant diseases
- **Powdery mildew** — white powder on leaves; improve **airflow**, avoid wetting foliage, remove affected leaves.
- **Pythium / root rot** — brown, slimy, foul-smelling roots; usually **low root-zone oxygen or warm water**. Raise DO,
  cool the water, remove affected plants.
- **Damping-off** — seedlings collapse at the soil line; caused by wet, stagnant, over-warm starts.

## Golden rule
If you wouldn't pour it in the fish tank, don't let it drip into the system. Physical + biological control keeps
plants, fish, and biofilter all alive.

# Choosing an irrigation method

_Domain: irrigation_
_Evidence: guidance_
_Applicability: General method comparison; requires site assessment and local trials before selecting equipment._
_Sources: [FAO Irrigation Water Management, Training manual 5, provisional edition, chapter 7](https://www.fao.org/4/s8684e/s8684e08.htm)_

## Compare the whole job

No method wins on every farm. Compare soil intake and storage, slope, crop, climate,
water quality and availability, labour, maintenance skills, equipment access and cost.
Local trials help establish suitability. These are selection factors in FAO's method
comparison, not a ranking of technologies.

Surface irrigation depends on field preparation and how water moves and infiltrates.
Sprinklers need pressure and are affected by wind. Drip requires attention to emitter
blockage and filtration. A method that can be maintained locally may be more practical
than equipment with unavailable parts or skills. Compare installation and operating
costs as well as expected benefits. See FAO chapter 7.

## Bring evidence to the comparison

Make a field sketch with dimensions, elevation changes, water entry and drainage.
Record the crop and season, supply availability, measured flow and pressure where
applicable, water-quality concerns, electricity or fuel access, labour and repair
options. Keep supplier quotes separate from measured performance. Mark unknowns.

In Agronaut, examine each capability's applicability before entering data. Current drip
design covers a flat layout with identical pressure-compensating emitters. Sprinkler
design covers a flat solid-set grid with a specified nozzle relation. Surface evaluation
analyzes a supplied event; automatic cutoff prediction is unavailable. Broader real-world
suitability does not extend these software models beyond their declared scope.

## Next step and limits

Use the drip, sprinkler or surface guide to prepare an observation record. Compare
candidate designs against the same sourced crop-water target and supply constraints.
Agronaut does not select a method from a country name or equate a feasible calculation
with a field-tested installation. Arrange a local review before using a design to buy
or operate equipment.

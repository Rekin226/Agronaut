# Aquaponic System Types and How to Choose One

The three classic designs (FAO 589) differ in how they hold plants and handle solids. Pick by your crops,
your scale, and your appetite for plumbing — not by what looks impressive.

## Media bed (flood-and-drain)
- Plants grow in a bed of inert **media** (expanded clay, lava rock, gravel), flooded and drained — often by a
  **bell siphon**.
- The bed does **three jobs at once**: mechanical filtration (traps solids), biofiltration (bacteria live on the media),
  and plant support. Simplest to run — no separate filters.
- Best for **beginners** and for **fruiting / larger plants** (tomato, pepper, cucumber) that need root anchorage.
- Trade-off: media is heavy and can clog with solids over time; lower plant density than raft at scale.

## Deep water culture (DWC) / raft
- Plants sit in floating **rafts** over deep channels of water; roots dangle in the flow.
- Needs **separate** mechanical filtration (to remove solids) and biofiltration — the water going under the rafts must
  already be clean, or roots foul.
- The **commercial standard for leafy greens** (lettuce, basil, herbs) at scale; large water volume buffers pH and
  temperature swings. This is the **UVI/Rakocy** design **Agronaut's sizing model is built around.**
- Trade-off: more components and footprint; less suited to heavy fruiting crops.

## Nutrient film technique (NFT)
- A thin **film** of water runs through narrow gutters; roots sit in the channel.
- **Space-efficient** and stackable/vertical — good where floor area is tight; suits leafy greens, herbs, strawberries.
- Needs separate filtration and is the **least forgiving on power loss** — the small water volume has little buffer, so
  roots dry and overheat quickly if flow stops. Not for beginners or unreliable power.

## Hybrid and vertical
- **Hybrid** — e.g. a media bed for solids + biofiltration feeding a raft/NFT growing section; common at mid scale.
- **Vertical / tower** systems maximise plants per floor area but demand steady pumping and even water distribution.

## Quick chooser
- **Beginner, mixed crops, want it simple** → media bed.
- **Commercial leafy greens, want density and buffering** → DWC / raft.
- **Tight space or vertical, herbs/greens, reliable power** → NFT or vertical.
- **Fruiting crops (tomato, pepper)** → media bed, or a hybrid with strong root support.

## Sizing note
Agronaut currently sizes the **raft/DWC** configuration (tank, biofilter, pump, water balance). The feed-per-plant-area
rule (FRR) still anchors a media bed, but media beds handle solids in-bed and use bed *volume* rather than raft area —
treat model numbers as a starting point when designing a media or NFT build.

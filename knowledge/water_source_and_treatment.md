# Source Water and Top-Up: What to Check Before It Reaches the Fish

The water you add to the system is not neutral — chlorine, hardness, and dissolved minerals in your source can stall a
biofilter, lock out nutrients, or stress fish. Test your source **once, up front**, and know what you're topping up with.

## Chlorine and chloramine (the first thing to remove)
- **Municipal tap water usually contains chlorine or chloramine** — both kill nitrifying bacteria and gill tissue.
- **Chlorine** off-gasses: let water stand 24 h with aeration, or use a dechlorinator.
- **Chloramine does NOT off-gas** — standing won't remove it. Use a **dechlorinator that neutralises chloramine**, or a
  carbon filter. When in doubt, treat top-up water; a big untreated refill can crash the biofilter.

## Hardness — KH vs GH (they're different)
- **KH (carbonate hardness / alkalinity)** — the pH buffer. Keep ~4–6 dKH; see `ph_and_alkalinity`.
- **GH (general hardness)** — dissolved **calcium and magnesium**, which plants and fish need. Very soft source water can
  leave systems short of Ca/Mg (tip burn, deficiencies); very hard water raises pH and can lock out iron.
- Test both. Soft water often needs Ca/Mg supplementation; hard, high-pH water may fight you on pH in a new system.

## Salinity / EC / TDS
- A little salinity is harmless and even protective for fish, but **leafy greens are salt-sensitive** and salt accumulates
  as water evaporates and you top up. Watch **EC/TDS** creep over time; a partial water change resets it.
- Never use **water softener (ion-exchange) output** for top-up — it swaps calcium for sodium, starving plants and salting
  the system.

## Source-water options
- **Rainwater** — soft and chlorine-free (good), but low in KH/Ca — buffer and add calcium as needed.
- **Well water** — test for hardness, iron, and gas supersaturation; aerate before use.
- **RO (reverse osmosis)** — very pure; remineralise (KH + Ca/Mg) before use or plants starve.

## Quick source-water checklist
- Chlorine/chloramine present? → treat before adding.
- KH and GH? → know your buffer and your Ca/Mg baseline.
- EC/TDS climbing over weeks? → schedule a partial water change.
- Big top-up needed? → treat and add gradually, never a large slug of cold, untreated water at once.

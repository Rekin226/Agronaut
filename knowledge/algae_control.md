# Algae Control

Algae is opportunistic: give it **light + nutrients + water** and it grows. An aquaponic system has plenty of nutrients
and water, so the one lever you control is **light**. Block the light and most algae problems disappear.

## Why algae is a problem (not just ugly)
- **Competes with your plants** for the same nutrients (nitrate, iron, phosphorus) — algae blooms can starve crops.
- **Swings dissolved oxygen dangerously.** Algae produces oxygen by day but **consumes it at night**; a heavy bloom can
  drive a dawn DO crash and gasping fish (see `dissolved_oxygen_and_aeration`).
- **Clogs** filters, pumps, plumbing, and roots; string algae tangles in rafts and NFT channels.
- A sudden algae die-off (e.g. after shading) **rots and spikes ammonia** — remove it, don't just kill it in place.

## The two kinds
- **Green water** — single-celled algae suspending in the water, turning it pea-soup green. Usually means **light is
  hitting exposed water** (open tanks/channels).
- **String / filamentous algae** — hair-like mats on surfaces, media, and rafts; thrives in well-lit, nutrient-rich, slow
  spots.

## Prevention (block the light)
- **Cover exposed water** — lids on fish tanks and sumps, close the gaps between rafts, cover pipe runs and filter tops.
- **Shade** open channels; keep the grow beds themselves densely planted so the crops (not algae) capture the light.
- Reduce standing, slow, sunlit water where algae settles.

## Removal (when it's already there)
- **Physically remove** string algae by hand and clean surfaces; don't let a big mat die and rot in the system.
- Restore aeration to cover the day–night DO swing while you deal with a bloom.
- **Avoid algaecides** — most are toxic to fish and to the biofilter; light control and physical removal are the
  aquaponics-safe path.

## Quick checks
- Water turning green? → find and cover the exposed, sunlit water.
- Dawn gasping with a visible bloom? → aerate now; the algae is stealing overnight oxygen.
- String algae in the rafts? → remove by hand, shade the channels, don't let it rot in place.

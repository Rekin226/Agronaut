# Solids Handling and Mineralization

Fish produce two waste streams: **dissolved** waste (ammonia → the nitrogen cycle) and **solid** waste (uneaten feed and
faeces). The nitrogen cycle handles the first. The solids are the part most beginners ignore — and they cause a
disproportionate share of failures.

## Why solids must be managed
- Accumulated solids **rot anaerobically**, consuming oxygen and releasing ammonia and hydrogen sulfide (rotten-egg smell)
  — a hidden driver of low DO and ammonia spikes (see `common_system_failures`).
- They **clog** pumps, plumbing, biofilter media, and plant roots, cutting flow and filtration.
- In raft/NFT systems especially, solids reaching the grow beds **foul the roots**.

## Mechanical filtration (remove solids early)
- **Media beds** handle solids in-bed — the media traps them, and worms/bacteria break them down. Simplest design, but the
  bed slowly loads up and eventually needs cleaning.
- **Raft (DWC) and NFT need a separate solids filter** *before* the water reaches the plants:
  - **Swirl / vortex separator (clarifier)** — water spins, heavier solids settle out the bottom to be drained.
  - **Screen / mesh / radial-flow filters** — catch fine particles; need regular rinsing.
- Whatever the method, **clean it on a schedule** — a solids filter only works if you actually empty it.

## Mineralization (turning solids back into nutrients)
- Captured solids still hold **nutrients the plants want** — especially phosphorus, potassium, and micronutrients that the
  dissolved stream is often short on.
- **Mineralization** = letting captured solids break down (aerobically, in a separate mineralization tank) so those
  nutrients dissolve and can be returned to the system. It recovers fertiliser you'd otherwise throw away and helps close
  the classic K/P/micronutrient gap (see `plant_nutrient_deficiencies`).
- Keep a mineralization tank **aerated** — anaerobic sludge produces toxic, foul water, not usable nutrients.

## Sludge disposal
- Solids you don't mineralize are excellent **garden compost / fertiliser** — nutrient-rich. Don't just bin them.

## Quick checks
- Rotten-egg smell or dark, greasy sludge? → anaerobic solids building up; remove and aerate.
- Flow dropping, filter overflowing? → clean the mechanical filter.
- Plants short on K/P despite feeding? → consider mineralizing captured solids back in.

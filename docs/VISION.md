# Agronaut vision

Updated 2026-10-18.

Agronaut's ambition is to become “The GOD of Agriculture”: an agricultural intelligence
platform that understands a farmer's operation, helps design it, guides decisions through
the season, and learns from real outcomes. Its practical promise is:

> Understand your farm. Plan with evidence. Act with clarity. Learn from results.

Aquaponics and hydroponics are the first specialties. Agronaut will expand into irrigated
and rainfed crops, greenhouses, orchards, and eventually other agricultural enterprises.
The destination is one assistant that can reason across an entire farm while using the
appropriate scientific models and local knowledge for each production unit.

## What farmers should be able to do

- Explain their land, climate, water, equipment, budget, and goals in ordinary language.
- Compare suitable production options and understand the constraints behind each option.
- Design systems and obtain inspectable calculations, equipment requirements, and plans.
- Decide what needs attention using weather, crop stages, measurements, and recorded actions.
- Investigate problems through possible causes and the observations that distinguish them.
- Record harvests, costs, and outcomes, then improve the next cycle with that evidence.
- Coordinate scarce water, pump time, labor, and budgets across different production units.

A farm may combine an aquaponic installation, hydroponic greenhouse, drip-irrigated tomato
field, sprinkler-irrigated maize, and rainfed crops. Agronaut must remember each separately
and account for resources they share. Production system, growing environment, irrigation
method, and crop are separate concepts.

## Principles that should survive every expansion

1. **Compute consequential numbers with inspectable models.** The language model gathers
   context, routes tools, and explains results; deterministic engines own calculations.
2. **Make evidence visible.** Inputs and coefficients have units, provenance, applicable
   conditions, and source versions. Results distinguish observations, estimates, and forecasts.
3. **Earn each capability.** Sourced guidance, verified calculations, experimental simulations,
   and field-validated operation are different claims. Validation applies to tested conditions.
4. **Learn from outcomes without erasing uncertainty.** Record what was recommended, what the
   farmer actually did, and what happened; separate calibration from independent evaluation.
5. **Meet farmers where they work.** Preserve self-hosting, offline calculations with supplied
   data, accessible language, voice and photo input, and existing messaging channels.
6. **Preserve farmer control.** Advice, approval, and performed actions are separate records.
   Hardware control is a later capability with explicit device and failure handling.
7. **Make contribution useful.** Agronomists, irrigation specialists, growers, translators,
   and software contributors all have concrete work to contribute.

The ambition describes breadth and usefulness. Each release must accurately describe what
Agronaut can calculate and where evidence remains incomplete.

## How the vision becomes a product

Start with a shared farm model and domain-aware consultations. Deliver one complete field
workflow: crop-water requirements and drip-irrigation planning for a bounded crop and region.
Validate it with operators. Add sprinkler and surface irrigation with their own calculations
and evidence. Build seasonal crop management, then coordination across the whole farm.

Expand into additional enterprises when there is a useful farmer workflow, a domain owner,
adequate sources, and a way to evaluate results. Existing aquatic specialties continue to
receive maintenance throughout that expansion.

Success means farmers return because Agronaut helps them make better decisions, and the
recorded outcomes support that usefulness. Count completed workflows, resource use, crop
outcomes, and retained users alongside the number of supported specialties.

The [agriculture platform plan](AGRICULTURE_PLATFORM_PLAN.md) defines milestones, architecture,
dependencies, and release gates. [GitHub epic #78](https://github.com/Rekin226/Agronaut/issues/78)
is the contributor entry point and tracks the implementation issues.

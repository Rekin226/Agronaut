## Not recommended (42)

Molecular biology and genomics (virus cloning, GWAS, bacterial community sequencing), IoT/ML monitoring papers (the known off-topic magnet), marine species (seaweed, nori, mariculture), and off-scope hits from over-broad regional queries (El Niño, birds, pigs, global hydrology models).

## What the harvest taught about the scopes

- `west_africa`, `east_asia` and `farm_economics` matched on the region or on 'profitability' alone. They need a topic pin or a stricter AND before the next run.
- 62 of 69 ACCEPTs came through Europe PMC; publisher PDFs mostly answered 403 or a bot challenge ('Access Denied', 'Just a moment').
- Recommended total: 1455 chunks from 21 sources, versus 3,941 today.

## Why WorldFish specifically

Of the entire multilateral landscape, the CGIAR **Asia Digital Hub at WorldFish** (Penang)
is the closest fit: it combines an **aquatic-foods mandate** with an explicit remit to
**"test, incubate and scale digital public goods."** Agronaut sits exactly at that
intersection — a digital public good for aquatic-food production. No other node pairs those
two mandates.

## The one-line pitch

> Agronaut is an open-source (MIT) conversational agronomy agent that turns FAO's small-scale
> aquaponics guidance into a **computed, cited, calibratable** design — delivered to
> smallholders over Telegram, WhatsApp, and the web, in text, photo, or voice.

## What makes it distinctive (and de-risked for a funder)

- **A deterministic, source-cited engineering core**, not an LLM guessing numbers. Every
  figure traces to a published coefficient (FAO 589, UVI/Rakocy); every design lists what it
  does **not** model. This directly answers the central funder concern that "wrong AI advice
  harms farmers" — the advice layer is verifiable, and a golden-set safety check is scored
  each release.
- **To our knowledge the first MIT digital implementation of FAO Fisheries & Aquaculture
  Technical Paper 589** — the manual, made computable.
- **A calibration loop that builds a public dataset**: real measured outcomes tune each
  operator's future designs and, in aggregate, form a cited record of small-scale system
  performance — a durable public good.
- **DPG-ready**: privacy policy, data export/erasure, platform independence (self-hostable
  open-weights), and a 9-indicator evidence map are all in-repo.

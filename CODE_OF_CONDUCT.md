# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Agronaut exists to help people grow food. We want contributing to feel like being helped by a
knowledgeable colleague — so we pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of experience level, education, background, nationality, appearance, disability,
age, gender identity, sexual orientation, or religion.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct and kind. Technical disagreement is welcome; contempt is not.
- Assume the other person knows something you don't. Many contributors here are practising
  farmers rather than programmers, and their field experience is worth more than fluency in
  Python.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Say when you're unsure. This project's whole premise is honesty about what is and isn't known.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal or political attacks, insults, discriminatory language, sexualised
language or imagery, publishing others' private information without permission, and sustained
disruption.

**Also specific to this project:** knowingly contributing agronomic advice you cannot support,
or misrepresenting a source. This project's value rests entirely on its citations. Fabricating
one is the most serious form of bad faith here, because someone may lose a crop or a tank of
fish to it.

## Scope

Applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at **abdoulrachid03@gmail.com**. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers who don't enforce this in good faith may be removed from
the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may warn, edit or remove contributions, or ban a participant temporarily or
permanently, depending on severity.
